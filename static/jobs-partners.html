Recruitment & Referral Partners

Discover Philippine and international programs offering candidate-hire or employer-referral commissions. Programs are external; registration and payment are handled by the partner.

Partner approval required. WOW SUPER APP is not affiliated with or endorsed by these providers. Listed amounts are advertised terms, not guaranteed earnings.

Before sharing: Read current partner terms and obtain approval. Never charge jobseekers for referrals or promise employment. For overseas recruitment, work only with appropriately licensed agencies and follow DMW rules. Do not upload applicant IDs, CVs or private data to this page.
